Delete your Stella Fit account

App: Stella Fit  ·  Last updated: August 14, 2026

Use this page to request deletion of your Stella Fit account and related data, as required for Google Play.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Stella Fit on your device.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

This signs you out, clears Stella Fit data stored on that device, and deletes your Firebase Authentication account when you signed up with email and password.

Delete on-device data without deleting your account

  1. Open Stella Fit → Profile.
  2. Tap Clear on-device data and confirm.

This removes wellness logs (fasting, sleep, weight, workouts, and related local data) but keeps your sign-in. Full instructions: Delete data.

If you signed in with Google

Stella Fit uses Google Sign-In for authentication. After deleting in the app (or if you no longer have the app), you can also revoke Stella Fit access in your Google Account:

  1. Open Google Account → Third-party access.
  2. Find Stella Fit (or the related OAuth client) and remove access.

What is deleted

  • Your Stella Fit Firebase Authentication account (email/password), when one exists
  • Local Stella Fit session on the device where you confirm deletion
  • On-device wellness logs on that device (fasting, sleep, weight, workouts, alarms, settings stored by Stella Fit)

What is not stored by Stella Fit on a server

Stella Fit does not keep a separate cloud copy of your fasting, sleep, weight, or workout logs. Those stay on your device unless you use your device’s own backup features.

Retention

In-app account deletion is processed promptly when the request succeeds (typically immediately). We do not retain your wellness logs on a Stella Fit backend after deletion because they are not uploaded to one.

If Firebase or Google retains security/audit records under their own policies, that is governed by Google’s terms for Firebase Authentication / Google Accounts — not a Stella Fit wellness database.

Need help?

Open an issue on the Stella Fit GitHub repository with the subject “Account deletion request” and the email used to sign in. We aim to complete manual requests within 7 days.

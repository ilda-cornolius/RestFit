Delete your RestFit data (keep your account)

App: RestFit  ·  Last updated: August 14, 2026

Use this page to delete wellness data stored by RestFit without deleting your account. This link is provided for Google Play Data safety disclosures.

Delete data in the app (recommended)

  1. Open RestFit on your device.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Under Account, tap Clear on-device data.
  4. Confirm the prompt.

Your sign-in stays active. Only on-device wellness logs and related local settings are cleared.

Other ways to remove on-device data

  • Clear RestFit’s app storage in your device settings, or
  • Uninstall RestFit (removes local data subject to device backup behavior)

What is deleted

  • Fasting history and protocol preferences stored by RestFit
  • Sleep and weight entries
  • Workout plans, notes, and session history
  • Alarms, reminders preferences, and other RestFit settings stored on the device
  • Local profile fields RestFit keeps for wellness tracking (for example target weight)

What is kept

  • Your RestFit account sign-in (email/password Firebase account and/or Google Sign-In session as applicable)
  • Account email / user ID held by Firebase Authentication or Google for sign-in

Retention

Clearing data in the app takes effect immediately on that device. RestFit does not upload wellness logs to a RestFit server, so there is no separate cloud wellness copy for us to retain after you clear local data.

Want to delete your account too?

See Delete account for steps to remove your RestFit login and local data together.

Need help?

Open an issue on the RestFit GitHub repository with the subject “Data deletion request” and the email used to sign in. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Join Stella Fit on Android

Two quick steps — join the tester group, then install from Google Play.

Stella Fit is in closed testing. Join the tester Google Group with the same account you use on your Android phone, then install from the Play testing link.
Group link not working? Ask the developer to add your Gmail in Play Console, or make sure you’re signed into Google with the account they invited. The group is stella-fit@googlegroups.com.
  1. 1

    Join the Google Group

    Tap below and request to join (or accept the invite). Use the same Google account you’ll use on your Android phone for the Play Store.

    Group: groups.google.com/g/stella-fit · stella-fit@googlegroups.com

    Join Google Group

    If you see “Content unavailable”, sign in with the Google account you were invited with, or ask for your email to be added as a tester.

  2. 2

    Install from Google Play

    After Google approves your group membership (usually right away), open the Play Store testing link on your phone and tap Become a tester, then install.

Join Stella Fit on Android

Two quick steps — join the tester group, then install from Google Play.

Stella Fit is in closed testing. Use the same Google account on this page and on your Android phone.
  1. 1

    Join the Google Group

    Tap the button below. Sign in with Google if asked, then request to join (or accept the invite).

    Join Google Group

    Group: groups.google.com/g/stella-fit · stella-fit@googlegroups.com

    If you see “Content unavailable”, switch to the Google account you use on your phone, or ask to be added as a tester.

  2. 2

    Install from Google Play

    After you’re in the group, tap below. On the Play page, choose Become a tester, then Install.

    Open Google Play
